# SOFT APP — Support | الدعم

**SOFT APP** is the mobile companion for offices subscribed to **Soft System — Financial Management**. Authorized staff can view office balances, customers, movements, reports and alerts. Depending on their role and permissions, they can also use internal staff chat and record or correct card-service bookkeeping operations. The app does not provide consumer financial services and has no public registration.

## English support

For help with sign-in, device approval, alerts, reports, internal chat, or card-operation records:

1. Contact your office administrator first for staff-account activation, permissions, branch access, or device approval.
2. Email **husseinzawin@gmail.com**.
3. WhatsApp **[+964 786 060 1660](https://wa.me/9647860601660)**.
4. Or open a **[new support request](https://github.com/husseinzwain/soft-app-support/issues/new)**.

Please include the app version, device model, and a description of the problem. **Do not post passwords, secret codes, receipt images, customer data, card numbers, identity numbers, or other office-confidential information in a public GitHub issue.** Use email or WhatsApp when private details are necessary.

Staff accounts are created and managed by the subscribing office; there is no self-registration. To deactivate an account or request access to, correction of, or deletion of associated data, contact your office administrator or use the private support channels above. Deactivation ends app access immediately; data-deletion requests are handled according to the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md) and applicable retention requirements.
